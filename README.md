## 🎯 Overview

The **Piercing Angle & Depth Guide** is a comprehensive professional reference tool designed exclusively for licensed body piercing professionals. This interactive calculator provides precise anatomical angles, insertion depths, jewelry specifications, safety protocols, and refuse unsuitable anatomy guidelines for 30+ different piercing types.

### Key Features

✅ **30+ Professional Piercings** - Comprehensive coverage of ear, facial, body, and genital piercings
✅ **Precise Technical Specifications** - Optimal angles, depths, tissue types, healing times
✅ **Jewelry Specifications** - Gauges, lengths, types, downsize timelines
✅ **Anatomical Positioning** - Detailed placement guidelines for each piercing
✅ **Safety Protocols** - Comprehensive safety considerations and contraindications
✅ **Refuse Unsuitable Anatomy** - Clear criteria for when to refuse piercings
✅ **Aftercare Guidelines** - Professional aftercare protocols for each piercing type
✅ **Professional Ethics** - Emphasis on safety, training, and informed consent
✅ **Dark Mode** - Eye-friendly dark mode for studio environments
✅ **Mobile Responsive** - Works on all devices
✅ **Free Embed** - Embeddable widget for training sites

## 🚨 CRITICAL: PROFESSIONAL USE ONLY

**THIS TOOL IS FOR LICENSED PROFESSIONAL PIERCERS ONLY**

Body piercing involves significant health risks and requires:

- Professional training and certification
- Sterile equipment and techniques
- Compliance with health department regulations
- Understanding of anatomy, aseptic technique, and bloodborne pathogens

**DO NOT attempt piercings without proper professional training.** Attempting body piercings without professional education can result in serious injury, infection, nerve damage, excessive bleeding, scarring, and transmission of bloodborne pathogens.

If you are not a licensed piercer, seek services only from certified professional piercers in licensed studios.

## 📊 For Each Piercing Type

Every piercing includes comprehensive professional data:

### Anatomical Specifications

- **Optimal Angle** - Precise angle through tissue
- **Insertion Depth** - Expected depth through tissue
- **Tissue Type** - Cartilage, soft tissue, membrane, etc.
- **Healing Time** - Expected healing timeline

### Jewelry Specifications

- **Initial Gauge** - Recommended starting gauge
- **Initial Length** - Jewelry length with swelling accommodation
- **Jewelry Type** - Barbell, CBR, labret, surface bar, etc.
- **Downsize Timeline** - When to downsize to proper-fit jewelry

### Professional Guidelines

- **Anatomical Positioning** - Detailed placement instructions
- **Safety Considerations** - Risks, contraindications, warnings
- **Refuse Unsuitable Anatomy** - When and why to refuse
- **Aftercare Protocols** - Professional aftercare guidelines
