## What is Piercing Angle Placement Guide?

The Piercing Angle Placement Guide is a free interactive reference tool that provides professional piercers with optimal angle, depth, and jewelry specifications for 25+ body piercing types. When a user selects a piercing type from the button grid, the tool displays a specs grid with eight data points: optimal angle, insertion depth, tissue type, healing time, jewelry gauge, jewelry length, jewelry type, and downsize time. Below the grid, four detail tabs offer expanded guidance on positioning, safety considerations, when to refuse the piercing based on anatomy, and aftercare protocols. The tool is built with JavaScript and HTML, pulling from a comprehensive dataset of industry-standard piercing specifications. It is designed for professional use and includes specific anatomical contraindications to help piercers make informed decisions about client suitability.

## Who Should Use This Guide

**Professional Body Piercers** - Use as a quick-reference during consultations and piercing procedures to verify angles, depths, and jewelry specifications.

**Experienced Piercers** - Refresh knowledge on less common piercings or verify specifications for piercings you perform infrequently.

## How to Use

1. **Select a Piercing Type** - Click any button in the piercing selector grid. Options are organized by body area: ear, facial, body, and genital piercings. The selected button highlights in purple.

2. **Review the Specs Grid** - After selection, eight specification cards appear showing optimal angle, insertion depth, tissue type, healing time, jewelry gauge, jewelry length, jewelry type, and downsize time.

3. **Explore Detail Tabs** - Click "Positioning," "Safety," "When to Refuse," or "Aftercare" tabs below the grid to access expanded guidance for the selected piercing.

4. **Assess Client Anatomy** - Use the angle and depth data as a baseline, then adjust based on individual client anatomy. The "When to Refuse" section provides specific contraindications.

5. **Document Your Assessment** - Reference the guide during consultations to explain placement decisions and anatomical limitations to clients.
